## Natural-text exploratory track

XQuAD by Mikel Artetxe, Sebastian Ruder and Dani Yogatama. Source: https://github.com/google-deepmind/xquad

Paper: *On the Cross-lingual Transferability of Monolingual Representations*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.11856

Pinned source commit: 7d30520c717524000f0d9d2f9c10a069acd9d285. Original file checksum is retained in every XQuAD case's source metadata and in `data/xquad-pilot-source.json`.

Changes: selected 20 Vietnamese question/passage pairs across 20 topics, made evidence-removed variants by deleting sentences containing reference answers, and added provisional answerability labels and grouped splits. Negative labels were screened with AI assistance but not independently human-reviewed. Selection is curated and not a representative random sample.

XQuAD and these derived cases remain under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Full license in `XQUAD-LICENSE.txt`.

The dataset authors do not endorse or validate this extension.

Ollama: https://ollama.com and https://docs.ollama.com/api/generate

Qwen2.5 1.5B: https://ollama.com/library/qwen2.5:1.5b and https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct-GGUF

Qwen3 8B: https://ollama.com/library/qwen3:8b and https://qwenlm.github.io/blog/qwen3/

Both evaluated snapshots are quantized local models; exact digests and quantization metadata are stored in the run manifest. Weights are not bundled.

## Related research

Tran et al. (2023), *Revealing Weaknesses of Vietnamese Language Models Through Unanswerable Questions in Machine Reading Comprehension*, EACL Student Research Workshop. https://aclanthology.org/2023.eacl-srw.1/

Asai et al. (2023), *Self-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection*. https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11511

These works motivate answerability and evidence evaluation. This project does not implement Self-RAG training/retrieval and does not claim to originate evidence-grounded QA or abstention.
